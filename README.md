# Landing Page Trio

Three original landing pages in one repository — built to show range. Each page targets a different industry, audience, and design direction, while using the same conversion fundamentals: a clear hero promise, proof, an obvious next step, and answers to the questions that stop people from taking it.

**Live demo:** https://brittanynation.github.io/landing-page-trio/

## The three pages

| Page | Concept | Industry / Audience | Design direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Project hub | Portfolio visitors & hiring managers | Neutral, editorial, quiet — the work speaks |
| `saas.html` | **Flowdesk** — team scheduling software | SaaS / B2B, operations leads at small teams | Indigo & violet, clean tech, airy: sticky nav, feature grid, pricing tiers, testimonial, FAQ accordion |
| `local.html` | **BrightPath Plumbing & Heating** | Local home services, homeowners | Orange & charcoal, trust-forward and practical: phone-first hero, services grid, reviews, service-area note, booking strip |
| `event.html` | **Neon Meadow Fest** — music festival | Events / entertainment, ticket buyers | Near-black with neon accents, bold condensed type: lineup, ticket tiers, schedule highlights, FAQ |

The three pages deliberately do **not** share a template. Layout, typography, density, and tone change with the audience — a homeowner with a burst pipe, a manager comparing software, and a festival-goer on their phone need three different pages.

## What it demonstrates

- **Range across industries** — SaaS, local services, and live events, each with its own visual language
- **Conversion-focused layout patterns** — hero + dual/single CTA matched to intent, social proof, pricing/ticket tiers, FAQ objection-handling, and repeated low-friction next steps
- **Responsive, mobile-first builds** — layouts designed for small screens first and enhanced at wider breakpoints
- **Semantic, accessible HTML** — landmarks, skip links, labelled sections, visible focus states, `aria-expanded` on interactive controls, and body-text contrast checked even on the dark/neon page
- **CSS-only visuals** — no image dependencies; gradients, borders, and type do the visual work

## Workflow note

My design workflow runs in **Sketch and Webflow**, where I design and build real projects. These demo pages were hand-built in HTML/CSS (with a small amount of vanilla JavaScript for the mobile nav toggle and FAQ accordions) so the underlying code is easy to review alongside the design.
